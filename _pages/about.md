I am a quantitative social scientist studying socio-economic inequalities across the life course, with a focus on family dynamics, poverty and gender inequality. My work draws on large-scale survey and administrative register data.

I am currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Sociology, [University of Vienna](https://www.soz.univie.ac.at/forschung/forschungsteams/team-steiber/). Previously, I held research positions at the University of Bamberg, the Vienna University of Economics and Business, and Statistics Austria.

I completed my PhD titled _Poverty risk within couples across the family life course_ at the University of Vienna in 2025 with distinction. My work has been recognized with the Theodor Körner Prize (2024), the Kurt Rothschild Prize (2025), the AK Wissenschaftspreis (2025) and the Best Dissertation Award from the Faculty of Social Sciences, University of Vienna (2025).
