I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair of Sociology, especially family and work, at the University of Bamberg and an external research fellow at the Department of Sociology, University of Vienna. Using large-scale survey and register data, my research focus lies at the intersection of family, work, and poverty research, with a particular focus on gender inequality across the life course.

I completed my PhD titled _Poverty risk within couples across the family life course_ at the University of Vienna in 2025 with distinction. My work has been recognized with the Theodor Körner Prize (2024), the Kurt Rothschild Prize (2025), and the Best Dissertation Award from the Faculty of Social Sciences, University of Vienna (2025).

Previously, I held research positions at the University of Vienna, Vienna University of Economics and Business, and Statistics Austria, and worked as a social policy expert at Diakonie Austria. I currently serve on the board of the _Social Inequality_ section of the Austrian Sociological Association (ÖGS), co-organize the Conference for Young Poverty Research as part of the Austrian Poverty Conference, and participate in the _Income and Wealth_ working group within the Austrian Socio-Economic Panel (ASEP).
