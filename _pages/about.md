I am a quantitative social scientist studying socio-economic inequalities across the life course, with a focus on family, gender, and poverty. My work draws on large-scale survey and administrative register data.

I am currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair of Sociology, especially family and work, at the [University of Bamberg](https://www.uni-bamberg.de/en/sociology/chairs-and-professorships/sfa/team/translate-to-english-dr-christina-siegert/) and I am an external research fellow at the Department of Sociology, [University of Vienna](https://www.soz.univie.ac.at/forschung/forschungsteams/team-steiber/). 

I completed my PhD titled _Poverty risk within couples across the family life course_ at the University of Vienna in 2025 with distinction. My work has been recognized with the Theodor Körner Prize (2024), the Kurt Rothschild Prize (2025), and the Best Dissertation Award from the Faculty of Social Sciences, University of Vienna (2025).
